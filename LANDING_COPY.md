## Objection Handling

### Objection: "I don't have time"
**Response**: "60 seconds. That's it."

### Objection: "My stack is already optimized"
**Response**: "Great! We'll confirm that. Most teams find at least one surprise."

### Objection: "I need these tools"
**Response**: "We never recommend removing tools you need. Just optimizing plans and eliminating overlap."

### Objection: "Savings seem too good to be true"
**Response**: "Our estimates are conservative. Real savings often exceed our recommendations."

### Objection: "What's the catch?"
**Response**: "No catch. Free forever. We might add premium features later, but the core audit stays free."

## Tone & Voice

### Brand Voice
- **Honest**: Never oversell, acknowledge limitations
- **Helpful**: Genuinely want users to save money
- **Smart**: Demonstrate expertise without jargon
- **Friendly**: Approachable, not corporate

### Writing Principles
- Short sentences
- Active voice
- Specific numbers over vague claims
- "You" language (not "we")
- Conversational but professional
