# Economics

## Business Model

### MVP Strategy (Day 1-7)
**100% Free** — Prove value before monetization

**Why Free First?**
- Build trust through demonstrated savings
- Collect usage data and feedback
- Validate product-market fit
- Generate word-of-mouth

---

## Value Proposition

### For Users
**Immediate Value**: Identify $100-500/month in AI tool savings in under 60 seconds

**No Risk**:
- No credit card required
- No signup required (initially)
- No commitment

**Honest Recommendations**:
- Sometimes "you're already optimized"
- Conservative savings estimates
- Financially defensible logic

## Revenue Potential (Post-MVP)

### Monetization Options

#### Option 1: Freemium Model
**Free Tier**:
- One-time audit
- Basic recommendations
- No historical tracking

**Premium Tier** ($10-20/month):
- Unlimited audits
- Historical tracking
- Team dashboards
- Savings alerts
- Priority support

**Target**: 1-5% conversion rate

#### Option 2: Lead Generation
**Model**: Free for users, charge tool vendors

**Revenue Sources**:
- Referral fees from tool providers
- Sponsored recommendations
- Affiliate commissions

**Concern**: Conflicts with honesty-first approach

**Verdict**: Avoid for MVP

#### Option 3: Enterprise SaaS
**Target**: Companies with 50+ employees

**Features**:
- Multi-team dashboards
- Usage analytics
- Budget forecasting
- Procurement integration

**Pricing**: $500-2000/month

**Timeline**: 6-12 months post-MVP

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors
**None identified** — No dedicated AI spend audit tools exist yet

### Adjacent Competitors
- **SaaS spend management**: Vertice, Vendr, Zylo
  - Focus: General SaaS, not AI-specific
  - Pricing: Enterprise-focused ($$$)
  
- **Expense tracking**: Expensify, Brex
  - Focus: General expenses, no optimization logic
  
- **Procurement**: Zip, Tropic
  - Focus: Buying process, not optimization

### Competitive Advantage
1. **AI-specific expertise** — Deep knowledge of AI tool pricing
2. **Deterministic logic** — Trustworthy, explainable recommendations
3. **Free tier** — Low barrier to entry
4. **Speed** — 60-second audit vs. hours of manual analysis

## Risk Factors

### Market Risks
- **AI tool pricing changes frequently** — Requires constant updates
- **Consolidation** — Fewer tools = less optimization opportunity
- **Free tiers improve** — Less need for paid alternatives

### Product Risks
- **Recommendations ignored** — Users don't trust or act on advice
- **Complexity creep** — Product becomes too complicated
- **Competition emerges** — Larger players enter space

### Mitigation Strategies
- Automated pricing updates (post-MVP)
- Focus on honesty and trust
- Keep product simple and focused
- Move fast, establish brand early
